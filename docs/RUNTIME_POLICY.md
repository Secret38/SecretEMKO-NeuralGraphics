# Runtime policy

`SecretEMKO.asi`, the open-source backend code, and required open-source notices can be built/distributed under their applicable licences.

These are separate proprietary NVIDIA components and are **not automatically redistributed by the public build**:

```text
nvngx_dlss.dll
nvngx_dlssnr.dll
nvngx_dlssg.dll
NVIDIA Streamline production binaries
```

The build may use upstream tooling to obtain supported Streamline files after the user reviews/accepts NVIDIA's terms.

Do not take proprietary DLLs out of PureDark or another paid mod and publish them as part of Secret EMKO.

Keeping runtimes separate also allows the NVIDIA model/runtime version to be changed without recompiling the ASI.
